# Brand voice article

Draft publishable copy that sounds like the brand and stays true to the sources.

## When to use
The user has a topic and wants a first draft that needs light editing, not a rewrite.

## Inputs to collect
- Audience and the one thing they should do or understand after reading.
- Brand voice in 3 to 5 adjectives, plus two sentences that sound right and two that do not.
- Sources the article may rely on. Facts outside them must be marked for checking.
- Length and format.

## Steps
1. Write a one-line promise for the article and a working title.
2. Build an outline of 3 to 6 sections; each section answers one question the reader has.
3. Draft with active voice, concrete examples and short paragraphs. Open with the most useful point, not background.
4. Mark every statistic or claim with its source in brackets. Anything without a source gets `[check]`.
5. Do a voice pass: remove filler, replace general words with specific ones, cut anything that sounds like every other article.
6. Add a title, a meta description under 155 characters and two alternative headlines.

## Output format
The article, then a list of `[check]` items, then the metadata.

## Quality checks
- No invented quotes, numbers or customers.
- The reader knows what to do after the last paragraph.
