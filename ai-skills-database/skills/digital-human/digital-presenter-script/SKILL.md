# Digital presenter script

Write a script that works when spoken by a virtual presenter, and is honest about what the presenter is.

## When to use
The user wants a video or livestream segment presented by an AI avatar or voice.

## Inputs to collect
- Topic, audience, target length and platform.
- Presenter persona: name, tone, how they greet and sign off.
- Visual assets available (slides, screen recordings, images).

## Steps
1. Open with a hook in the first 5 seconds that tells viewers what they will get.
2. Split the script into segments of 15 to 40 seconds. For each: spoken lines, on-screen text, visual, and duration.
3. Write for the ear: short sentences, no brackets, numbers spelled the way they should be said, one idea per line.
4. Add a plain disclosure near the start, for example "I'm an AI presenter" or the platform's required label.
5. Add pauses and emphasis marks only where the voice tool needs them.
6. End with one call to action and a short recap line.

## Output format
A table with `Time | Spoken line | On-screen text | Visual`, then the disclosure line and the total running time.

## Quality checks
- Read aloud, no line takes more than one breath.
- The presenter never claims human experiences, credentials or real customers.
- Total time matches the target within 10 percent.
