# Lead research brief

Prepare a short, honest brief about a company before anyone contacts them.

## When to use
Someone is about to reach out to a prospect and needs context in minutes, not hours.

## Inputs to collect
- Company name and website.
- What we offer and who it is for (one sentence).
- Which public sources are allowed (website, press, job posts, public filings).

## Steps
1. Read the company's own site first: what they sell, to whom, in which markets.
2. Look for signals of need in public sources: hiring, new products, funding, leadership changes. Note the source and date for each.
3. Score fit as **strong / possible / weak** and give two sentences of reasoning. If information is missing, say so instead of guessing.
4. Identify the most likely role to contact (by function, not by inventing a name).
5. Draft a first message of under 90 words: one specific observation, one relevant benefit, one low-pressure question.

## Output format
`Company snapshot` (3 bullets), `Signals` (with sources), `Fit`, `Who to contact`, `Draft message`.

## Quality checks
- No private or scraped personal data; only public business information.
- Every signal has a source and a date.
- The draft contains no claim we cannot back up.
